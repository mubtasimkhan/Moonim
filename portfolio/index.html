About Me

Hi, I’m Muhammad Mubtasim Moonim Khan, a tech enthusiast from Bangladesh with a strong curiosity about what happens behind the screen.

I enjoy exploring technology hands-on, from managing servers and experimenting with Linux to building projects, optimizing setups, and learning how different systems work together. I’m particularly interested in cybersecurity, system administration, networking, and the endless possibilities of technology.

Outside of tech, music is a big part of my everyday life. I’m an avid listener and a bit of an audiophile, always discovering new artists, sounds, and ways to get the most out of my music. My taste ranges from Linkin Park and Foo Fighters to Martin Garrix, Luísa Sonza, and Bad Bunny.

I’m also proud to have served as an Ambassador for the ICT Olympiad Bangladesh, where I had the opportunity to be involved in the technology community and connect with people who share the same curiosity and passion for learning.

I’m always looking for something new to build, something interesting to understand, or a problem worth solving. My goal is to keep learning, keep experimenting, and gradually turn that curiosity into deeper expertise in cybersecurity and technology.
